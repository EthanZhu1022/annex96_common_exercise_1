\chapter{Preliminary Evaluation and Planned Analysis}\label{ch:eval}

This chapter reports the current partial results and the remaining evaluation
plan. The results should be treated as preliminary because they are based on
Vermont only, one random seed, and 200 training episodes. They are useful for
debugging, early comparison, and identifying promising directions, but they are
not sufficient for final claims.

中文：本章报告当前阶段性结果与剩余评估计划。
由于结果仅基于 Vermont、单个随机种子和 200 训练回合，因此应视为初步结果。
它们可用于调试、早期比较和识别有潜力方向，但不足以支撑最终结论。

\section{Experimental Setting}

The experiments use the Annex 96 Common Exercise 1 CityLearn portfolios. Each
portfolio contains 25 single-family homes with photovoltaic generation and
battery storage. Two climates are available: Vermont, which is
heating-dominated, and Texas, which is cooling-dominated. The current completed
test results are for Vermont only.

中文：实验采用 Annex 96 Common Exercise 1 的 CityLearn 组合。每个组合包含 25 栋配有光伏发电和电池储能的独立住宅。可用气候有两类：以采暖为主的 Vermont 与以制冷为主的 Texas。当前已完成的测试结果仅包含 Vermont。

The available runs use seed 42 and 200 training episodes or iterations. Most

\section{Primary Metrics}

The primary objective is portfolio-level reference tracking. Let $y_t$ be the
actual aggregate portfolio load and $r_t$ be the reference load. The two primary
metrics are:

中文：首要目标是组合层参考曲线跟踪。设 $y_t$ 为实际组合聚合负荷，$r_t$ 为参考负荷。两个核心指标如下：

\begin{equation}
\mathrm{NMBE} =
\frac{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(y_t-r_t)}
{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}r_t} \times 100\%,
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV\mbox{-}RMSE} =
\frac{\sqrt{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(y_t-r_t)^2}}
{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}r_t} \times 100\%.
\end{equation}

NMBE measures systematic bias relative to the target. CV-RMSE measures overall
tracking error and is the most important single number for the load-following
objective. Comfort exceedance hours are also included because tracking
improvement is not useful if it is achieved by sacrificing indoor comfort.

中文：NMBE 衡量相对目标的系统性偏差。CV-RMSE 衡量整体跟踪误差，是负荷跟随目标最关键的单一指标。舒适性超限小时也被纳入，因为若以牺牲室内舒适性换取跟踪改善，则这种改善并无实际意义。

The early results show several useful patterns. SB3 independent PPO has the
lowest CV-RMSE, but its portfolio reward and comfort performance are much
worse than the MAPPO variants. This suggests that its tracking improvement is
not currently balanced with comfort or reward. Standard MAPPO has the best
reward sum and lowest comfort exceedance among the available MAPPO-style runs,
but its CV-RMSE is not the best. This indicates that the reward and the Annex
96 tracking metric are related but not identical.

中文：早期结果呈现出若干有价值模式。SB3 独立 PPO 的 CV-RMSE 最低，但其组合奖励与舒适性表现明显差于各 MAPPO 变体，说明其跟踪改进尚未与舒适性或奖励形成平衡。标准 MAPPO 在当前 MAPPO 类运行中具有最佳奖励总和与最低舒适性超限，但 CV-RMSE 并非最优，表明奖励与 Annex 96 跟踪指标相关但并不等价。

Among the communication variants, global CommNet is currently the most
promising. It improves CV-RMSE from 55.71\% for the no-communication wrapper to
53.05\%, while also improving reward from -41856.6 to -36186.4. TarMAC also
improves CV-RMSE relative to the no-communication wrapper, but not as strongly
as CommNet at 200 episodes. DIAL-style communication performs poorly in the
current run. The weighted group communication method does not yet show the
expected benefit; it may need more training, coefficient tuning, or a different
message normalization.

中文：在通信变体中，全局 CommNet 当前最有前景。其将 CV-RMSE 从无通信封装的 55.71\% 降至 53.05\%，同时将奖励从 -41856.6 提升到 -36186.4。TarMAC 相比无通信封装也改善了 CV-RMSE，但在 200 回合下改善幅度不及 CommNet。DIAL 风格通信在当前运行中表现较差。组内/组间加权通信尚未体现预期收益，可能需要更长训练、系数调优或不同的消息归一化方式。

\section{Current Interpretation}

The current evidence supports three cautious conclusions. First, the MAPPO
pipeline is functioning and produces substantially better reward and comfort
than the available SB3 independent PPO run. Second, communication is not
automatically beneficial: different communication mechanisms produce different
trade-offs, and DIAL-style or weighted communication are not better under the
current 200-episode configuration. Third, global CommNet is the strongest
communication candidate at this stage because it improves both reward and
CV-RMSE relative to the no-communication wrapper.

中文：当前证据支持三条审慎结论。第一，MAPPO 流水线能够稳定运行，并在奖励与舒适性上显著优于当前可用的 SB3 独立 PPO。第二，通信并非天然有益：不同通信机制会产生不同权衡，在现有 200 回合配置下，DIAL 风格或加权通信并未更优。第三，全局 CommNet 是本阶段最强通信候选，因为其相对无通信封装同时改善了奖励与 CV-RMSE。

These are not final conclusions. The 200-episode runs should be treated as
evidence for selecting next experiments, not as proof of algorithm superiority.

中文：以上并非最终结论。200 回合运行应被视为“下一步实验选择”的证据，而非“算法优越性已被证明”的结论。
