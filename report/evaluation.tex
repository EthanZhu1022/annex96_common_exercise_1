\section{Primary and Secondary Metric Overview}

The primary objective is portfolio-level reference tracking. Let $y_t$ be the
actual aggregate portfolio load and $r_t$ be the reference load. The two main
tracking metrics are NMBE and CV-RMSE:

中文：首要目标仍然是建筑组合层面的负荷跟踪。记 $y_t$ 为实际组合负荷，
$r_t$ 为参考负荷。核心跟踪指标仍然是 NMBE 和 CV-RMSE：
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{NMBE} =
\frac{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(y_t-r_t)}
{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}r_t} \times 100\%,
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV\mbox{-}RMSE} =
\frac{\sqrt{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}(y_t-r_t)^2}}
{\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T}r_t} \times 100\%.
\end{equation}

Lower absolute NMBE and lower CV-RMSE mean better tracking. Comfort exceedance
hours are reported together with them because a controller that tracks the
target well but causes too many thermal violations is still not practically
useful.

中文：$|$NMBE$|$ 越低、CV-RMSE 越低，说明跟踪越好。与此同时，还必须同时看
舒适性超限小时数，因为如果一个控制器虽然跟踪好了目标，但造成大量温度越界，
它在实际中仍然不算好方案。

\section{Current Interpretation and Remaining Limits}

The updated Vermont benchmark results support four main conclusions. First, MARL
is useful for this task. The stronger grouped MAPPO variants clearly outperform
RBC and also avoid the very poor comfort trade-off seen in SAC. Second,
communication matters, but the best communication is not fixed in advance. The
new data no longer supports a simple ``\texttt{comm\_v2} is best'' statement;
instead, different communication structures dominate under different ranking
rules. Third, if the question is the all-round composite ranking on the current
selected Vermont set, the best controller is now grouped MAPPO with PowerNet
Global communication. Fourth, the weighted communication ablation shows that the
message-combination rule itself is a meaningful optimization direction.

中文：更新后的 Vermont 结果支持四条主要结论。第一，MARL 对这个任务是有帮助的。
较强的分组 MAPPO 变体明显优于 RBC，也避免了 SAC 那种很差的舒适性代价。
第二，通信确实重要，但“哪种通信最好”并不是一个固定答案。新数据已经不再支持
“\texttt{comm\_v2} 一定最好”的简单说法，而是说明不同通信结构会在不同排序规则下
占优。第三，如果问题是当前 Vermont 结果集中的综合排名，那么最优方法现在是带
PowerNet Global 通信的分组 MAPPO。第四，加权通信消融说明，消息组合规则本身
就是一个值得继续优化的研究方向。

The remaining limits are now more specific than ``the runs are not finished''.
The selected Vermont runs are complete, but the comparison still uses one seed
and one climate. Texas RL experiments, multi-seed validation, and cross-family
comparisons with methods such as MASAC or value-based MARL are still needed
before making broader claims about the full research question. Cost and carbon
metrics also remain unavailable because the CE1 schemas used here do not supply
pricing or carbon-intensity files.

中文：当前剩下的限制，已经不再是“实验还没跑完”，而是更具体的研究设计问题。
选定的 Vermont 结果已经完整，但当前对比仍然只使用一个随机种子，也只覆盖一个气候场景。
在对完整研究问题做更广泛结论之前，仍然需要完成 Texas 的 RL 实验、多随机种子验证，
以及与 MASAC 或基于价值函数方法的跨家族比较。与此同时，由于当前 CE1 schema
没有提供电价和碳排放强度文件，成本和碳排放指标仍然不能解释。
