\chapter{Method and Progress}\label{ch:method}

This chapter follows the same grouped MAPPO and centralized-training,
decentralized-execution foundation used in the previous report. The new work is
concentrated in the actor communication block, the construction of building
groups, and the selection of actor experts. This stage also investigates which
reward-function weight setting works best. The basic reward form is retained,
but several combinations of its weights are compared to study the balance
between load tracking and indoor comfort.

\begin{zhtranslation}
本章沿用上一份报告中的 grouped MAPPO 以及“集中训练、分散执行”基础。新的工作主要集中
在 actor 通信模块、建筑分组构造和 actor expert 选择上。本阶段也探索研究了最佳的奖励函数参数权重：
奖励函数的基本形式保持不变，但对多组权重参数进行了对比实验，用来研究负荷跟踪与
室内舒适度之间的平衡。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Reward Function and Parameter Comparison}

At time step $t$, let $y_t$ be the total electricity load of all $N$
buildings, let $r_t$ be the target load, and let $e_t=y_t-r_t$ be the tracking
error. The two running load-tracking terms used by the reward are
\begin{align}
B_t &= \left|\frac{\sum_{\tau=1}^{t}e_\tau}
{\sum_{\tau=1}^{t}r_\tau}\right|,\\
E_t &= \frac{\sqrt{\frac{1}{t}\sum_{\tau=1}^{t}e_\tau^2}}
{\frac{1}{t}\sum_{\tau=1}^{t}r_\tau},
\end{align}
where $B_t$ is the running absolute NMBE term and $E_t$ is the running
CV-RMSE term. NMBE shows whether the total load is generally above or below the
target over time: a positive value means that it is generally too high, while a
negative value means that it is generally too low. The reward uses its absolute
value, so a result closer to zero means less overall bias. CV-RMSE shows
the typical size of the hour-by-hour tracking errors relative to the average
target load, and larger errors have a stronger effect on it. A lower CV-RMSE
therefore means that the load follows the target more closely. For building $i$,
the number of degrees outside the seasonal comfort range $[L_t,U_t]$ and the
corresponding violation indicator are
\begin{align}
d_{i,t} &= \max(L_t-T_{i,t},0)+\max(T_{i,t}-U_t,0),\\
I_{i,t} &= \mathbb{I}[d_{i,t}>0].
\end{align}
The complete per-building reward is therefore
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:report2-reward}
R_{i,t}=-\left[
\frac{w_{\mathrm{nmbe}}B_t+w_{\mathrm{cv\_rmse}}E_t}{N}
+w_{\mathrm{comfort}}
\left(w_{\mathrm{binary}}I_{i,t}+w_{\mathrm{degree}}d_{i,t}\right)
\right].
\end{equation}
The first two penalties are calculated from the total load and divided equally
among the $N$ buildings. The final term is calculated separately for each
building. It gives one penalty when the indoor temperature leaves the comfort
range and an additional penalty according to how many degrees it exceeds the
range. In the reported experiments, Vermont uses January for training and
February for testing, with a comfort range of $20$--$24^{\circ}$C. Texas uses
August for training and September for testing, with a comfort range of
$22$--$26^{\circ}$C.

The five commonly used parameters are the NMBE weight
$w_{\mathrm{nmbe}}$, CV-RMSE weight $w_{\mathrm{cv\_rmse}}$, overall comfort weight
$w_{\mathrm{comfort}}$, fixed comfort-violation weight
$w_{\mathrm{binary}}$, and temperature-exceedance weight
$w_{\mathrm{degree}}$. In this order, the usual baseline setting is
$(1.0,1.0,0.8,1.3,0.3)$. Reward-parameter comparison experiments were also
conducted. The tested settings included a comfort-focused combination
$(1.0,1.0,1.5,3.0,1.5)$ and a load-tracking-focused combination
$(5.0,5.0,1.0,1.0,1.5)$. These comparisons test whether increasing the load
weights or the comfort weights produces a better balance in the final results;
therefore, the reward parameters were also tuned rather than treated as fixed.

\begin{zhtranslation}
在时间步 $t$，令 $y_t$ 表示 $N$ 栋建筑的总用电负荷，$r_t$ 表示目标负荷，
$e_t=y_t-r_t$ 表示跟踪误差。奖励函数中的 $B_t$ 是截至当前时间的绝对 NMBE，
$E_t$ 是截至当前时间的 CV-RMSE。NMBE 用来判断总负荷在一段时间内整体偏高还是偏低：
正值表示整体偏高，负值表示整体偏低。奖励函数使用它的绝对值，因此越接近零表示整体
偏差越小。CV-RMSE 用来表示每个时间点的负荷通常偏离目标多少，并且
较大的误差会对它产生更明显的影响；因此，CV-RMSE 越低，说明负荷曲线跟随目标曲线越好。
对建筑 $i$，$d_{i,t}$ 表示室内温度超出当季舒适范围 $[L_t,U_t]$ 的度数，$I_{i,t}$
表示室内温度是否超出该范围。因此，完整奖励函数如公式~\eqref{eq:report2-reward} 所示。

其中，前两项根据所有建筑的总负荷计算，并平均分给 $N$ 栋建筑；最后一项则对每栋建筑
单独计算。室内温度一旦超出舒适范围，就会产生一次固定惩罚；超出的温度越多，还会产生
更大的额外惩罚。在本报告的实验中，VT 使用1月训练、2月测试，舒适温度范围为
$20$--$24^{\circ}$C；TX 使用8月训练、9月测试，舒适温度范围为 $22$--$26^{\circ}$C。

奖励函数常用的五个参数分别为 NMBE 权重 $w_{\mathrm{nmbe}}$、CV-RMSE 权重
$w_{\mathrm{cv\_rmse}}$、总体舒适度权重 $w_{\mathrm{comfort}}$、固定舒适度超限权重
$w_{\mathrm{binary}}$ 和温度超限度数权重 $w_{\mathrm{degree}}$。按照这个顺序，常用的
基线参数为 $(1.0,1.0,0.8,1.3,0.3)$。本阶段也对奖励参数做了对比实验，包括更重视
舒适度的 $(1.0,1.0,1.5,3.0,1.5)$，以及更重视负荷跟踪的
$(5.0,5.0,1.0,1.0,1.5)$。这些实验用于比较提高负荷权重或舒适度权重后，最终结果中的
负荷跟踪与室内舒适度能否取得更好的平衡。因此，奖励参数也经过了调参，而不是始终固定
使用同一组数值。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{TarMAC Hybrid Communication}

The original implementation follows targeted multi-agent communication
(TarMAC) \cite{tarmac2019}. For hidden state $h_i\in\mathbb{R}^{256}$, it forms
three representations. The query describes what information building $i$ is
looking for. The key describes what information each building can provide and
is used for matching. The value contains the actual information to be passed.
They are calculated as
\begin{align}
q_i &= W_q h_i, & k_i &= W_k h_i, & v_i &= W_v h_i,\\
a_{ij} &= \operatorname{softmax}_j\left(\frac{q_i^\top k_j}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right),
& c_i &= \sum_j a_{ij}v_j,
\end{align}
The model compares the query of one building with the keys of all buildings.
A better match receives a larger weight, and the values are combined according
to these weights to form the received communication information $c_i$. The
division by $\sqrt{d_k}$ only prevents the matching scores from becoming too
large. The original implementation then computes an update directly from the
complete local state and $c_i$. Because the full 256-dimensional local state
enters the fusion layer without compression, repeated local information can
overwhelm the 64-dimensional communication information in this 25-building task.

TarMAC Hybrid changes the fusion path to
\begin{align}
\ell_i &= \operatorname{ReLU}(W_{\ell}h_i), \qquad
\ell_i\in\mathbb{R}^{64},\\
\Delta_i &= \tanh\!\left(W_2\tanh\left(W_1[\ell_i;c_i]
+b_1\right)+b_2\right),\\
h'_i &= h_i + \Delta_i.
\end{align}
Compared with the original TarMAC communication method, TarMAC Hybrid makes
three main changes. First, a learned $256\to64$ projection
turns the local state into a more compact representation and reduces repeated
information. Second, ReLU provides nonlinear filtering so that useful patterns
in the current input are more clearly represented. Third, a residual connection
adds the communication result to the original state instead of replacing the
original state completely. Together, these changes first simplify each
building's local information and then combine it with information from other
buildings without losing the original input. The default ReLU experiment uses
32-dimensional queries and keys and 64-dimensional values. It uses one global
communication round, meaning that all 25 buildings exchange information once
before the action is produced, and each building can consider information from
every other building rather than only buildings in its fixed group.

To determine which part of TarMAC Hybrid produces the improvement, the
experiments also compare two alternatives with the default ReLU fusion: Linear
and Gated. They separate the effect of simple dimensional reduction from the
effect of controlling how much communication information is added.

Linear fusion compresses the local state from 256 dimensions to 64 dimensions
without applying ReLU, and then combines this result directly with the received
communication information. It is used to test whether dimensional reduction
alone is sufficient.

Gated fusion learns a set of weights between zero and one that act like
adjustable switches. Based on the local state and the received information,
these switches decide how much of each part of the communication information
should be added to the local state. It is used to test whether controlling the
amount of incoming information is more effective than direct fusion.

\begin{zhtranslation}
原始实现遵循 TarMAC 的定向多智能体通信。对 256 维隐藏状态，它生成 query、key 和
value 三种表示。query 表示当前建筑希望获得什么信息；key 表示每栋建筑能够提供什么
信息，用来与 query 进行匹配；value 则是真正需要传递的信息内容。模型会把一栋建筑的
query 与所有建筑的 key 进行比较，匹配程度越高，对应 value 获得的权重就越大。最后，
模型按照这些权重汇总所有 value，得到该建筑接收的通信信息 $c_i$。公式中的
$\sqrt{d_k}$ 只是为了防止匹配分数过大。原始实现随后直接把完整本地状态与 $c_i$ 结合
来生成更新结果。由于 256 维本地状态没有经过压缩就进入融合层，在这个 25 栋建筑的
任务中，重复的本地信息可能会压过 64 维通信信息。

TarMAC Hybrid 在原始TarMAC通讯算法基础上主要进行了三项改进。首先，使用可学习的 $256\to64$ 投影，把本地状态
转换成更紧凑的表示，减少重复和冗余信息。其次，使用 ReLU 进行非线性筛选，让当前输入
中的有效特征表现得更加清楚。最后，使用残差连接把通信结果加入原始状态，而不是直接
替换原始状态。这样，模型会先简化每栋建筑的本地信息，再将其与其他建筑的信息结合，
同时保留原始输入。默认的 ReLU 实验使用 32 维 query/key 和 64 维 value，并且只进行
一次全局通信，即在生成动作之前，全部 25 栋建筑共同交换一次信息；每栋建筑都可以参考其他所有建筑的
信息，而不只参考自己固定组内的建筑。

为了进一步判断 TarMAC Hybrid 的提升主要来自哪一部分，实验还在默认的 ReLU 融合之外
设置了 Linear 和 Gated 两种对照方式。它们分别用于区分单纯降维的作用，以及控制通信
信息加入量的作用。

Linear 融合只把本地状态从 256 维压缩到 64 维，不使用 ReLU，然后直接将压缩结果与
接收到的通信信息结合。这个设置用于检验只做降维是否已经足够。

Gated 融合会学习一组 0 到 1 之间的权重，可以把它们理解成能够调节大小的开关。模型
根据本地状态和接收到的信息，决定通信信息的各个部分应当加入多少。这个设置用于检验
控制外部信息的加入量，是否比直接融合更加有效。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Selectable Grouping Methods}

All methods receive the same standardized feature matrix $X$. For each candidate
number of groups $K\in\{4,5\}$, the implementation tries the requested method,
computes the balance score $\min_k n_k/\max_k n_k$, and selects the most balanced
candidate. K-means uses ten initializations. GMM uses full covariance matrices,
three initializations, and covariance regularization $10^{-6}$. Agglomerative
clustering uses Ward linkage and is deterministic.

The balanced spectral method first constructs an RBF affinity matrix on the
standardized features. Its kernel width is determined from the median positive
squared distance. It then computes the symmetric normalized graph Laplacian,
row-normalizes the eigenvectors associated with the smallest eigenvalues, and
uses K-means only to initialize embedding-space centroids. Each centroid is
expanded into an exact number of assignment slots, and the Hungarian algorithm
assigns all buildings to these slots. Group sizes therefore differ by at most
one. Equal-balance solutions are separated by the lower normalized-cut cost.

\begin{zhtranslation}
所有方法都接收同一标准化特征矩阵。对每个候选组数 $K\in\{4,5\}$，实现会运行指定方法，
计算平衡分数 $\min_k n_k/\max_k n_k$，并选择最平衡的候选结果。K-means 使用十次初始
化；GMM 使用完整协方差、三次初始化和 $10^{-6}$ 协方差正则；凝聚聚类使用 Ward
linkage，因此是确定性的。

Balanced spectral 先在标准化特征上构建 RBF 相似度矩阵，核宽度由正的平方距离中位数
决定；随后计算对称归一化图 Laplacian，并对最小特征值对应的特征向量逐行归一化。
K-means 只用于初始化谱嵌入空间中的中心，每个中心被展开为规定数量的分配槽，最后用
匈牙利算法把所有建筑分配到这些槽。因此，各组大小最多相差一栋；在平衡度相同时，
选择 normalized-cut cost 更低的结果。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Grouping Feature Design}

The full control-profile feature pool includes battery capacity, total HVAC
rating, mean and maximum heating demand, mean and maximum non-shiftable load,
mean occupancy, indoor-temperature variation, and comfort-bound exceedance.
Every selected column is standardized before clustering. Two ablation families
were implemented.

The feature-count ablation compares:
\begin{itemize}
  \item 3fA: battery capacity, mean heating demand, and mean non-shiftable load;
  \item 4f: 3fA plus total HVAC rating;
  \item 5f: 4f plus mean lower-comfort-bound exceedance.
\end{itemize}
The feature-combination ablation holds the count at three while replacing mean
with maximum demand, replacing thermal demand with HVAC rating, adding a comfort
or temperature statistic, or removing battery capacity. This design separates
the effect of dimensionality from the effect of physical content.

For Texas, the same feature roles are retained but
\texttt{heating\_mean} is replaced with \texttt{cooling\_mean}. Thus the
cross-climate experiment tests a transferable principle---capacity, controllable
thermal burden, and background load---rather than blindly copying a Vermont
heating feature into a cooling-dominated season.

\begin{zhtranslation}
完整的 control-profile 特征池包括电池容量、HVAC 总额定功率、平均和最大供暖需求、
平均和最大不可转移负荷、平均住户数、室温波动以及舒适下界超限。聚类前对所选列分别
标准化。实验包含两类消融：特征数量消融比较 3fA、4f 和 5f，其中 4f 在 3fA 上增加
HVAC，5f 再增加舒适下界超限；特征组合消融则固定为三个特征，通过 mean/max 替换、
用 HVAC 替代热需求、增加舒适度或温度统计以及移除电池容量，区分“维度数量”和“物理
内容”的影响。

在 Texas 中保留同样的角色结构，但把 \texttt{heating\_mean} 替换为
\texttt{cooling\_mean}。因此跨气候实验检验的是“容量、可控热负荷、背景负荷”这一
可迁移原则，而不是把 Vermont 的供暖特征机械复制到制冷主导季节。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{State-Conditioned Soft Router}

Let $K$ fixed group policies be experts. For building $i$ at time $t$, the
router receives the globally communicated hidden state and, in the capacity-
aware version, seven physical variables: normalized battery capacity,
normalized HVAC rating, state of charge, available charge headroom, available
discharge headroom, current non-shiftable load, and current heating demand. A
two-layer router produces logits $z_{it}$ and dynamic weights
\begin{equation}
d_{it}=\operatorname{softmax}(z_{it}/\tau).
\end{equation}
The final gate interpolates between the one-hot fixed assignment $p_i$ and the
dynamic gate,
\begin{equation}
g_{it}=(1-\alpha)p_i+\alpha d_{it},
\end{equation}
where $\alpha$ is increased during training. A balance penalty discourages
collapse to one expert by comparing average gate usage with the usage implied
by the original partition. Evaluation can use hard selection from the largest
gate while training remains differentiable.

\begin{zhtranslation}
设 $K$ 个固定分组策略为 experts。对时刻 $t$ 的建筑 $i$，router 接收全局通信后的隐藏
状态；在 capacity-aware 版本中还接收七个物理变量：归一化电池容量、归一化 HVAC
额定值、SOC、可充电余量、可放电余量、当前不可转移负荷和当前供暖需求。两层 router
输出 logits，再经温度 $\tau$ 控制的 softmax 得到动态权重。最终 gate 在固定 one-hot
分组 $p_i$ 和动态 gate $d_{it}$ 之间插值，$\alpha$ 随训练增加。balance penalty 通过
比较平均 expert 使用率与原固定分组的使用率来避免路由塌缩。训练保持可微，测试时可以
选择 gate 最大的 expert。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Router Training Sequence}

The soft-router experiments evolved through four designs:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{End-to-end legacy training.} Router and actors were trained
  together from randomly initialized policies. Temperature, warm-up duration,
  and final prior weight were varied. These runs established that a router could
  learn non-uniform gates, but policy learning was unstable.
  \item \textbf{Pretrained two-stage training.} A strong fixed 3fA actor
  checkpoint was loaded first. Experts were frozen for 200 episodes before
  limited joint fine-tuning, with temperature 0.5 or 0.7, final dynamic weight
  1.0 or 0.7, and with/without the seven capacity-aware router inputs.
  \item \textbf{Three-stage training.} Experiments separated static actor
  training, router-only learning, and dynamic actor adaptation. Both a shared-
  encoder version and a full-expert version were tested. The shared version
  attempted to put every head in one latent space; the full-expert version
  retained matched encoder--communication--head branches.
  \item \textbf{Stable two-stage enhancement.} The best fixed 3fA checkpoint
  was treated as an anchor. The most conservative run trained only the router
  for 500 episodes (\texttt{routeronly500}). The stronger stable-head family
  then allowed only action heads to adapt at $10^{-5}$ while encoders and TarMAC
  remained frozen and the router was frozen during the adaptation phase.
\end{enumerate}

This sequence tests the central question directly. If soft routing could replace
clustering, the end-to-end and shared three-stage runs should learn good experts
without a strong prior. If it is instead an enhancement, the pretrained and
stable variants should be better because they preserve a useful initial
partition.

\begin{zhtranslation}
Soft router 实验经历了四个设计阶段。第一，最初的 end-to-end 版本从随机 actor 开始，
同时训练 router 和 experts，并改变 temperature、warm-up 和 prior 权重；它证明 router
可以产生非均匀 gate，但策略不稳定。第二，两阶段版本先载入强 3fA 固定分组 checkpoint，
冻结 experts 200 episodes 后再有限联合微调，同时比较 temperature 0.5/0.7、最终动态
权重 1.0/0.7，以及是否使用七个 capacity-aware 输入。第三，三阶段版本明确分开静态
actor、router-only 和动态 actor 适应，并比较 shared-encoder 与 full-expert。第四，
稳定两阶段增强把最佳 3fA checkpoint 作为锚点；最保守的 \texttt{routeronly500} 只训练
router 500 episodes，而 stable-head 版本只允许 action heads 以 $10^{-5}$ 小学习率适应，
encoder 和 TarMAC 保持冻结，适应阶段 router 也冻结。

这一演进直接检验核心问题：如果 soft routing 能完全替代聚类，那么 end-to-end 和 shared
三阶段应该无需强先验就能学出好 experts；如果它只是增强方法，那么保留优质初始分组的
预训练与稳定版本应当表现更好。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Completed Implementation}

The current codebase now supports selectable clustering, arbitrary feature
columns, cluster summaries, hybrid fusion ablations, fixed and dynamic routing,
capacity-aware router inputs, router entropy and balance terms, feed-forward and
GRU routers, checkpoint-based staged training, per-stage freezing, and multi-
seed launch scripts. Each result directory exports the run configuration,
cluster assignment, checkpoint, primary and secondary metrics, daily traces,
comfort tables, training curves, and router history where applicable.

\begin{zhtranslation}
当前代码已支持可选聚类方法、任意特征列、cluster summary、hybrid fusion 消融、固定与
动态路由、capacity-aware router 输入、router entropy/balance 项、前馈与 GRU router、
基于 checkpoint 的阶段训练、分阶段冻结以及多随机种子脚本。每个结果目录都会导出运行
配置、分组、checkpoint、主次指标、逐日轨迹、舒适度表、训练曲线，以及适用时的 router
history。
\end{zhtranslation}
