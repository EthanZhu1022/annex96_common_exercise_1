\chapter{Conclusion and Work Plan}\label{ch:concl}

This report continued the first grouped-MAPPO study by asking how communication,
building grouping, and grouping features should be designed together for the
CityLearn portfolio load-tracking task, and whether the controller can reduce
its dependence on fixed grouping. The experiments show that all three design
choices affect the final policy. The strongest overall approach combines a
clearer TarMAC communication structure, compact building features,
agglomerative grouping, and a stable Soft Router.

\begin{zhtranslation}
本报告延续了第一阶段的 grouped-MAPPO 研究，重点回答两个问题：在 CityLearn 建筑组合
负荷跟踪任务中，应当如何共同设计通信结构、建筑分组方法和分组特征；以及控制器能否
减少对固定分组的依赖。实验结果表明，这三个方面都会影响最终策略。整体效果最好的方案
结合了更清楚的 TarMAC 通信结构、紧凑的建筑特征、凝聚聚类和 stable Soft Router。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Main Findings}

First, TarMAC Hybrid improves the original TarMAC design by compressing each
agent's local information, retaining the more useful parts with ReLU, combining
them with received communication, and preserving the original information
through a residual connection. In the matched benchmark, it reduces CV-RMSE
from 49.65\% to 47.01\%, comfort exceedance from 22.10\% to 17.99\%, and the
reward magnitude from 5996 to 4715. The linear and gated ablations confirm that
the improvement does not come from simply adding another layer.

Second, building grouping is part of the learning system rather than a neutral
preprocessing step. The four tested methods produce different results:
agglomerative clustering gives the strongest load tracking in the method
comparison, so it was selected for the later feature experiments.

Third, more grouping features do not necessarily improve performance. The 3fA
combination---battery capacity, mean heating demand, and mean non-shiftable
load---provides the best overall balance because it has low and stable bias,
clear physical meaning, and can be adapted from Vermont heating to Texas
cooling. Some other three-feature combinations are better on individual
metrics, but 3fA is the most suitable overall choice. Its results also show that
selecting a few features with different physical roles is more useful than
adding many similar or strongly related descriptions.

Finally, the stable-head Soft Router gives the strongest routing result. Across
three router-training seeds, it achieves CV-RMSE $45.12\pm0.35$\%, absolute
NMBE $1.35\pm0.33$\%, degree-hours $3.78\pm0.44$ per building-day, and comfort
exceedance $17.30\pm1.33$\%. Its mean load tracking and comfort severity are
better than those of the five-run fixed 3fA family. The staged training and
anti-collapse measures also keep the router from sending almost all buildings
to one actor.

\begin{zhtranslation}
第一，TarMAC Hybrid 对原始 TarMAC 进行了改进：先压缩每个智能体的本地信息，用 ReLU
保留更有用的部分，再与接收到的通信信息结合，同时通过残差连接保留原始信息。在相同
基准下，它将 CV-RMSE 从49.65\%降到47.01\%，将舒适超限从22.10\%降到17.99\%，并将
reward 绝对值从5996降到4715。Linear 和 gated 消融结果说明，性能提升并不是简单增加
一层网络带来的。

第二，建筑分组不是一个与训练无关的预处理步骤，而是学习系统的一部分。四种分组方法
产生了不同结果，但凝聚聚类在分组方法比较中具有最好的负荷跟踪结果，因此后续特征实验选择了
凝聚聚类。

第三，分组特征并不是越多越好。3fA 由电池容量、平均供暖需求和平均不可转移负荷组成，
它具有低且稳定的 bias、清楚的物理含义，并且可以从 Vermont 的供暖场景调整到 Texas 的
制冷场景，因此是最合适的综合选择。部分其他三特征组合在个别指标上更好，但 3fA 的整体
平衡更好。结果也说明，选择少量物理作用不同的特征，比加入许多相似或强相关的描述更有效。

最后，stable-head Soft Router 得到了最好的 routing 结果。在三个 router-training seeds
中，它的 CV-RMSE 为 $45.12\pm0.35$\%，绝对 NMBE 为 $1.35\pm0.33$\%，每栋建筑每天的
平均 degree-hours 为 $3.78\pm0.44$，舒适超限为 $17.30\pm1.33$\%。它的平均负荷跟踪
和舒适度严重程度都优于固定 3fA 的五次实验。分阶段训练和防坍塌措施也避免了 router 将
几乎所有建筑都交给同一个 actor。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Main Innovation}

The main innovation of this work is introducing a Soft Router into grouped
MAPPO to select a complete actor for each building according to its current
state. Under ordinary fixed grouping, a building always uses the actor assigned
to its group. With the Soft Router, the same building can use different actors
at different times. The final training process first keeps the pretrained
actors unchanged while learning the selector, and then fixes the learned
selector while allowing only small changes to the actor output heads.

This design reduces the restriction imposed by a permanent building-to-actor
assignment while retaining the useful behaviour learned by the fixed experts.
The stable multi-seed results show that it is a practical performance
enhancement rather than only a theoretical idea. Therefore, Soft Router can
reduce the model's dependence on fixed grouping, although it does not yet remove
that dependence completely.

\begin{zhtranslation}
本研究最大的创新点是在 grouped MAPPO 中加入 Soft Router，根据建筑当前状态为其选择
一个完整的 actor。在普通固定分组中，一栋建筑始终使用所属组对应的 actor；加入 Soft
Router 后，同一栋建筑可以在不同时刻使用不同 actors。最终训练过程先保持预训练 actors
不变，只学习负责选择 actor 的网络；然后固定已经训练好的选择器，只对 actor 的输出层
进行小幅调整。

这种设计减少了建筑与 actor 永久绑定造成的限制，同时保留固定 experts 已经学到的有效
控制能力。稳定的多 seed 结果说明，它不仅是理论想法，也是一种实际有效的性能提升方法。
因此，Soft Router 可以减少模型对固定分组
的依赖，但目前还不能完全消除这种依赖。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Limitations}

The main limitation is that the final Soft Router still depends on strong
pretrained actors. Its three reported seeds all start from the same seed-42
fixed-expert checkpoint and change only the seed used for later router training.
The results therefore show that the router can be trained consistently, but do
not show that the complete model can learn equally strong experts and routing
from different random initializations. The weaker end-to-end and shared-encoder
experiments confirm that the current router cannot yet replace fixed grouping.

The experimental coverage is also not fully balanced. Most intermediate Soft
Router variants use only seed 42 because their early results were poor and
repeated training has a high time and computational cost. These runs are useful
for selecting promising directions, but the final comparison still needs more
strictly paired seeds.

Finally, cross-climate testing currently covers only the 25-building Vermont
and Texas settings. The Texas controller is much better than the no-control and
RBC references, but its load bias remains high. Some buildings also continue to
violate the comfort range for long periods. The current conclusions therefore
need validation under more varied conditions.

\begin{zhtranslation}
目前最大的局限是，最终 Soft Router 仍然依赖效果较好的预训练 actors。报告中的三个
seeds 都从同一个 seed-42 固定 expert checkpoint 开始，只改变后续 router 训练使用的
随机种子。因此，结果能够说明 router 可以稳定训练，但不能说明整个模型从不同随机初始
状态开始时，也能学出同样有效的 experts 和 routing。较弱的 end-to-end 和 shared-encoder
实验也说明，当前 router 还不能完全替代固定分组。

实验覆盖也还不完全平衡。大部分中间 Soft Router 版本只使用 seed 42，因为它们的初步
结果较差，而且重复训练需要很高的时间和计算成本。这些实验足以帮助筛选值得继续研究的
方向，但最终比较仍需要更加严格的配对多 seed 结果。

最后，跨气候测试目前只覆盖由25栋建筑组成的 Vermont 和 Texas 场景。Texas 控制器明显
优于 no-control 和 RBC 参考，但负荷 bias 仍然较高；部分建筑也仍会长时间超出舒适温度
范围。因此，目前结论还需要在更多不同条件下进行验证。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Future Work}

The next stage should focus on strengthening the current conclusions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item continue studying stronger grouping methods and feature combinations to
  improve the earlier fixed-grouping performance;
  \item improve Soft Router training so that it can start from weaker groups or
  random initialization without routing collapse;
  \item analyse actor-selection probabilities and building-level comfort in
  more detail, and further improve the current method under different climate
  conditions.
\end{itemize}

These experiments will determine whether Soft Router can progress from a stable
enhancement of fixed grouping toward a more independent and adaptive grouping
method.

\begin{zhtranslation}
下一阶段应重点加强目前结论的验证：
\begin{itemize}
  \item 继续研究更优秀的分组方法和特征组合设置，提升前期固定分组性能；
  \item 继续改进 Soft Router，使其从较弱分组或随机初始化开始时也能训练，并避免 routing
  坍塌；
  \item 更详细地分析 actor 选择概率和每栋建筑的舒适度，进一步提高目前实验方法在不同气候条件下的表现。
\end{itemize}

这些实验将进一步判断 Soft Router 能否从固定分组之上的稳定提升方法，发展为更加独立、
更加灵活的动态分组方法。
\end{zhtranslation}
