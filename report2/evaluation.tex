\chapter{Evaluation and Result Analysis}\label{ch:eval}

This chapter evaluates the methods introduced in Chapter~\ref{ch:method} using
the CityLearn Annex 96 Common Exercise 1 portfolio load-tracking task. It
compares TarMAC Hybrid, alternative building-grouping methods, grouping-feature
ablations, Vermont--Texas generalization, and the Soft Router. The results are
analysed in terms of load tracking, thermal comfort, and stability across
multiple random seeds.

\begin{zhtranslation}
本章使用 CityLearn Annex 96 Common Exercise 1 建筑组合负荷跟踪任务，对第~\ref{ch:method}
章提出的方法进行评估。主要比较 TarMAC Hybrid、不同建筑分组方法、分组特征消融、
Vermont--Texas 泛化以及 Soft Router，并从负荷跟踪、热舒适度和多随机种子稳定性三个方面
分析实验结果。
\end{zhtranslation}

\section{Evaluation Scope and Metrics}

Vermont uses January for training/group-feature extraction and February for
testing. Texas uses August for training/group features and September for
testing. Unless otherwise stated, runs use 500 training episodes. Load tracking
is reported using CV-RMSE and the absolute value of NMBE. Comfort is reported as
both the percentage of building-hours outside the seasonal comfort band and
degree-hours per building-day, which preserves the magnitude as well as the
duration of a temperature violation while making results comparable across test
periods and portfolio sizes. It is calculated as
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:degree-hours-per-building-day}
\mathrm{DH}_{\mathrm{bd}}
=\frac{\text{portfolio total degree-hours}}
{N_{\mathrm{building}}\,D_{\mathrm{test}}}
=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\mathrm{building}}}\sum_{t=1}^{T}d_{i,t}\Delta t}
{N_{\mathrm{building}}\,D_{\mathrm{test}}},
\end{equation}
where $d_{i,t}$ is the number of degrees by which building $i$ is outside the
comfort band at time $t$, $\Delta t$ is the time-step duration,
$N_{\mathrm{building}}$ is the number of buildings, and $D_{\mathrm{test}}$ is
the number of test days. Its unit is $^\circ$C h per building-day. Lower values
are better for all four metrics.

The evaluation combines repeated training results from multiple random seeds
across the main feature, climate, and Soft Router studies.
Controlled ablations compare runs under the same seed and settings, while
repeated runs are summarized by their mean and standard deviation to verify
that the main conclusions do not depend on one unusually good result. An
individual run is discussed only when it is needed to explain a specific
controlled comparison.

\begin{zhtranslation}
Vermont 使用一月进行训练和分组特征提取、二月测试；Texas 使用八月训练和提取特征、
九月测试。除非另有说明，每个实验训练 500 episodes。负荷跟踪使用 CV-RMSE 和 NMBE
绝对值；舒适度同时使用超限 building-hours 比例和 degree-hours，后者同时保留温度越界
的幅度与持续时间。本报告使用的是每栋建筑每天的平均超标度时，即组合总超标度时除以
建筑数量与测试天数的乘积，如公式~\eqref{eq:degree-hours-per-building-day} 所示。其中
$d_{i,t}$ 是建筑 $i$ 在时间 $t$ 超出舒适温度范围的度数，$\Delta t$ 是时间步长度，
$N_{\mathrm{building}}$ 是建筑数量，$D_{\mathrm{test}}$ 是测试天数。该指标的单位为
$^\circ$C h/建筑--天。四个指标均越低越好。

本章综合使用了多次不同随机种子的重复训练结果，覆盖主要的特征、气候和 Soft Router
实验。控制变量的消融实验在相同 seed 和相同设置下进行比较；需要判断稳定性时，则使用
多次重复实验的均值和标准差，确保主要结论不是由某一次偶然较好的结果决定。只有在解释
某个具体的控制对照时，才会单独讨论一次实验结果。
\end{zhtranslation}

\subsection{Router-Collapse Analysis}

Routing collapse occurs when the router sends almost all decisions to one
expert, leaving the other actors with little or no use. Table~\ref{tab:router-collapse}
uses the recorded training history of two matched seed-42 full-expert runs to
examine this directly. Both use temperature $\tau=0.5$, increase $\alpha$ from
0.5 to 1.0 over 150 episodes, use entropy coefficient $c_H=0.05$, balance
coefficient $c_B=0.01$, and train the router at $10^{-4}$. Their first 500
router-only episodes are identical. After this point, the joint version resumes
router updates after a 100-episode pause and updates the full experts at
$3\times10^{-5}$; the stable version freezes the router for all remaining 500
episodes and updates only the action heads at $10^{-5}$.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\scriptsize
\begin{center}
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|l|r|r|r|}
\hline
Recorded point & $\tau$ & $\alpha$ & $c_H$ & $c_B$ & Router status & Gate entropy & Mean largest gate & Largest expert share \\
\hline
Start, episode 1 & 0.5 & 0.50 & 0.05 & 0.01 & training & 1.223 & 0.602 & 30.6\% \\
End of router-only, episode 500 & 0.5 & 1.00 & 0.05 & 0.01 & training & 0.275 & 0.891 & 36.4\% \\
Joint adaptation, episode 1000 & 0.5 & 1.00 & 0.05 & 0.01 & updated & 0.171 & 0.934 & 51.7\% \\
Stable head adaptation, episode 1000 & 0.5 & 1.00 & 0.05 & 0.01 & frozen & 0.276 & 0.891 & 37.0\% \\
\hline
\end{tabular}}
\end{center}
\caption{Matched seed-42 routing-concentration data. Higher entropy and lower
largest-gate/share values indicate less concentration.}
\label{tab:router-collapse}
\end{table}

With five experts, the largest possible gate entropy is $\log(5)=1.609$. The
prior and gradual $\alpha$ schedule give initial expert shares of 21.9\%,
20.1\%, 30.6\%, 13.3\%, and 14.1\%. At episode 500 the largest share is still
only 36.4\%. Continuing joint training then reduces entropy from 0.275 to 0.171
and raises one expert's share to 51.7\%, whereas freezing the router preserves
entropy at 0.276 and limits the largest share to 37.0\%. The three final stable
seeds have largest shares of 37.5\%, 40.5\%, and 37.0\%, and the matched seed-42
stable run also improves CV-RMSE from 45.45\% to 44.72\% and degree-hours from
6.39 to 4.27 per building-day. Together with probability sampling and PPO
clipping, this supports
the combined use of the prior, gradual $\alpha$, entropy, balance, and staged
freezing. It does not produce perfect balance: expert 3 receives only
0.015--0.036\% of final traffic. The safeguards therefore prevent total
one-expert collapse, but one branch can still become almost unused.

\begin{zhtranslation}
Router 坍塌是指 router 把几乎所有控制选择都交给同一个 expert，导致其他 actor 很少或完全
不被使用。表~\ref{tab:router-collapse}使用两组相同 seed 42 的 full-expert 实验记录直接分析
这一问题。两组实验都使用 $\tau=0.5$，在150个 episodes 内把 $\alpha$ 从0.5增加到1.0，
entropy 系数为 $c_H=0.05$，balance 系数为 $c_B=0.01$，router 学习率为 $10^{-4}$，并且
前500个 router-only episodes 完全相同。之后，joint 版本先暂停更新 router 100个 episodes，
随后继续联合更新 router 和完整 experts，expert 学习率为 $3\times10^{-5}$；stable 版本则在
后500个 episodes 始终固定 router，只用 $10^{-5}$ 的学习率调整 action heads。

五个 experts 的最大 entropy 是 $\log(5)=1.609$。原固定分组 prior 和逐步增加的 $\alpha$
使 episode 1 的五个 expert 使用比例保持在21.9\%、20.1\%、30.6\%、13.3\%和14.1\%。到
episode 500，最大比例仍只有36.4\%。继续联合训练会使 entropy 从0.275降到0.171，并使一个
expert 上升到51.7\%；固定 router 后，entropy 保持在0.276，最大比例只有37.0\%。三个最终
stable seeds 的最大比例分别为37.5\%、40.5\%和37.0\%，而 matched seed-42 stable 实验还将
CV-RMSE 从45.45\%降到44.72\%、每栋建筑每天的平均 degree-hours 从6.39降到4.27。这说明组合防坍塌设计有效，
包括 prior、逐步增加 $\alpha$、entropy、balance、按概率抽样、PPO clipping 和分阶段冻结。
不过 expert 3 最终只占0.015--0.036\%，说明这些措施能避免所有选择集中到单个 expert，
但还不能完全避免某一个分支几乎不被使用。
\end{zhtranslation}

The final stable-head result is the clearest evidence that routing has value.
Across router-training seeds 0, 1, and 42, CV-RMSE is $45.12\pm0.35$\%,
degree-hours per building-day are $3.78\pm0.44$, and comfort exceedance is
$17.30\pm1.33$\%. All three runs load the same strong seed-42 fixed-expert
checkpoint, so this measures stability of routing and limited head adaptation
\emph{conditional on one expert anchor}; it is not an end-to-end multi-seed
test of expert learning. The fixed 3fA family over all five available seeds has
CV-RMSE $49.86\pm3.65$\% and degree-hours per building-day of
$4.64\pm1.27$. These sets are not
paired, but the smaller conditional router variance and improved means justify
calling the stable router a useful enhancement. Reward is not equally stable:
one stable-head seed has a reward sum of $-17225$ despite acceptable reported
primary metrics, so the result should be presented as strong but not fully
resolved.

The five available \texttt{routeronly500} runs give CV-RMSE
$49.83\pm3.34$\%, $|$NMBE$|$ $1.19\pm0.60$\%, degree-hours
$4.55\pm1.25$ per building-day, and comfort exceedance $18.73\pm2.43$\%.
Router-only training is
therefore safer than uncontrolled joint training, but its mean gain over fixed
3fA is modest. The larger gain appears when stable pretrained branches are kept
and only a narrowly scoped head adaptation is allowed.

\begin{zhtranslation}
表~\ref{tab:router-progression}汇总主要阶段。除最后一行是三 seeds 均值外，其余使用
seed 42。Legacy end-to-end 的 CV-RMSE 尚可，但 bias 和 degree-hours 较高；载入预训练
3fA experts 后，bias 立即改善。Shared-encoder 三阶段是最差的路由结果，说明在训练
router 时重新组织 expert 表示会破坏已经有用的专门化；full-expert 更好，因为 encoder、
communication 和 head 始终保持匹配。

最终 stable-head 是 soft routing 有价值的最清晰证据。router-training seeds 0、1、42
的 CV-RMSE 为 $45.12\pm0.35$\%，每栋建筑每天的平均 degree-hours 为 $3.78\pm0.44$，舒适超限为
$17.30\pm1.33$\%。但三次实验都载入同一个强 seed-42 固定 expert checkpoint，因此

\section{Overall Interpretation and Limitations}

The experiments support four conclusions. First, TarMAC Hybrid is a meaningful
architectural improvement, and its advantage is best explained by compact local
fusion plus a residual communication correction. Second, grouping is part of
the learning system rather than neutral preprocessing: K-means, GMM,
agglomerative, and balanced spectral partitions create different accuracy--
comfort trade-offs. Third, compact physically distinct features are preferable
to indiscriminately adding correlated building descriptors. 3fA is selected for
its very low bias, interpretation, and climate transfer, while 3fI remains an
important competing result on CV-RMSE and comfort. Fourth, soft routing can
improve performance and seed stability, but only after useful fixed experts
exist.

The last point answers the router research question cautiously. The current
soft router does not remove dependence on grouping. End-to-end and shared-
representation attempts do not learn a strong partition from scratch, whereas
pretrained full-expert and stable-head variants do. The router is therefore a
``pro'' extension of fixed grouping: it lets the policy switch or interpolate
between established specializations according to state, but the quality of the
initial grouping still sets the quality of the expert basis.

\begin{zhtranslation}
实验支持四点结论。第一，TarMAC Hybrid 是有意义的结构改进，其优势最好解释为紧凑本地
融合加残差通信修正。第二，分组不是中性的预处理，而是学习系统的一部分；K-means、
GMM、凝聚和 balanced spectral 会形成不同的精度--舒适度折中。第三，相比无差别加入
相关建筑描述，紧凑且物理作用不同的特征更合适；3fA 因低 bias、可解释性和气候迁移被
选中，但 3fI 在 CV-RMSE 与舒适度方面仍是重要竞争结果。第四，soft routing 能提高性能
和 seed 稳定性，但前提是已经存在有效的固定 experts。

因此，soft router 目前并没有消除对分组的依赖。End-to-end 和 shared-representation
尝试无法从零学出强分组，而 pretrained full-expert 与 stable-head 可以。Soft router
更适合被定位为固定分组的“pro”扩展：它允许策略随状态在已经建立的专门化之间切换或
插值，但初始分组质量仍然决定 expert 基础的质量。
\end{zhtranslation}
